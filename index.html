CHEF PORTRAIT COMING SOON
CHEF ARCHIE SPEIGHTS · FOUNDER & CULINARY CREATOR

SERVICE. FAITH. FAMILY. FLAVOR.

Made to serve.
Inspired to nourish.

Chef Archie Speights brings the same dedication to his kitchen that has shaped his life: a spirit of service and a genuine love for people. A combat veteran of the United States Army, he proudly served six years in uniform.

Beyond the kitchen, Archie is a pastor, a devoted husband, a father of three and a man whose faith guides the way he lives and serves. He believes a shared meal can spark conversation, build relationships and bring comfort to a room.

Currently pursuing undergraduate culinary studies at Auguste Escoffier School of Culinary Arts, he continues refining his knowledge of technique, ingredients and the layered flavors that make every dish memorable.

Beside him is his wife, Angela Speights, who supports what she affectionately calls his “food ministry”—helping manage the business behind the scenes and serving as a trusted taste tester. Together, they bring heart, hospitality and purpose to Spot or Blemish Catering.

Good to know.

How far in advance should I inquire?

Contact us as early as possible for preferred dates; availability and lead time depend on the size and complexity of your event.

Can you cater more than 75 guests?

Yes, larger events may be possible by special request and will receive a tailored proposal.

Do you accommodate food allergies or dietary needs?

Please include all restrictions in your inquiry. We will review feasibility and discuss ingredients and cross-contact limitations before confirming.

Are delivery and staffing included?

They are optional and priced separately based on distance, equipment, staff count and service time.

How are bookings confirmed?

We will provide a written proposal, payment terms, cancellation policy and any required deposit before an event is confirmed.
